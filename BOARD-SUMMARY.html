The Business Problem

BaSuka Energy's public email addresses were silently failing: inquiries from partners, investors, and applicants never arrived, and replies sent from company addresses bounced. Meanwhile, the website's contact forms relied on a free third-party service and WhatsApp redirects with no delivery guarantee. The result: missed meetings, unanswered partnership requests, and lost revenue opportunities.

What Was Done

  1. Email restored & secured. Mail routing was moved from broken forwarding to the company's own hosted mail system, protected with industry-standard authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC). Every address at basukaenergy.com now receives mail reliably, and outgoing mail is digitally signed — no more bounces at Gmail, Outlook, or corporate servers.
  2. A dedicated address per business function. Partnerships, academy applications, general inquiries, and the CEO's office each route to their own professional mailbox — nothing is lost, and every message is answerable directly.
  3. Website forms rebuilt on owned infrastructure. All five forms (contact, newsletter, partner inquiry, two academy applications) now deliver directly into company mailboxes through BaSuka's own server — zero third-party dependencies — with spam protection, rate limiting, and honest error messaging.
  4. Verified end to end. Every mailbox and every form was tested live in production; submissions arrived in each inbox.

What This Means for the Business

Ongoing Cost

$0PER MONTH

The system runs entirely on the company's existing assets — its domain (Namecheap), hosting (cPanel), and website platform (Vercel). Recommended upkeep is routine only: mark first emails as "not spam" and include mail in periodic hosting backups.
